Cancellation Policy

Last updated: 28 October 2026

Order cancellation requests

Contact Mr Mobiles as soon as possible and include your order number. Requests received before dispatch will be reviewed promptly. Orders already dispatched may need to follow the returns process.

Refunds

Refund eligibility and processing timelines depend on the order status, payment method and applicable law. Any cancellation charges must be disclosed and legally permissible.

Repair appointments and jobs

Repair appointments or jobs may be cancelled by contacting our support team. Charges for work already performed or parts specially ordered will be handled according to the terms agreed with the customer and applicable law.

Contact

Email contact@mrmobiles.in or use our Contact page. Nothing in this policy limits mandatory consumer rights.
