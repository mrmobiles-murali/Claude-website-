FRP (Factory Reset Protection) is a security feature that locks an Android phone to its last Google account after a factory reset. If you bought a used phone, inherited one, or forgot your own login, the phone becomes unusable until that lock is verified or removed. We handle this safely and always ask for proof of ownership first.
