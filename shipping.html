Shipping & Delivery Policy

Last updated: 28 October 2026

Order processing

Orders are processed after order confirmation, stock verification and payment confirmation where applicable.

Delivery estimates

Estimated dispatch and delivery dates will be communicated during ordering or confirmation. Delays may occur due to courier operations, weather, service availability or other circumstances outside our reasonable control.

Shipping charges

Applicable shipping and handling charges will be communicated before order confirmation.

Delivery address

Customers are responsible for providing a complete and accurate delivery address and a reachable contact number.

Damaged, missing or incorrect items

Please report damaged, missing or incorrect items promptly with your order details and supporting photographs where possible. Reporting requirements do not override statutory consumer rights.

Tracking and support

Tracking information will be shared where available. For assistance, email contact@mrmobiles.in or visit our Contact page.

This policy is subject to applicable law and the confirmed order terms.
